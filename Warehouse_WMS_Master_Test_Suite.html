Core Architecture & Operating Principles:
  • Secure Single Panel Login: Authenticated via 4-digit PIN (1947) or USB Key Token (wms-token.key).
  • Base Unit Inventory Rule: All GRNs & Dispatches deal strictly in minimum product units (e.g. 100 Gatta × 6 = 600 Pieces). Loose quantity dispatches (e.g. 6 pcs) reduce exact units (600 - 6 = 594 Pcs).
  • 6 Physical Shades Layout: 6 dedicated zones (Grains, Oils, FMCG, Packaging, Chemicals, Spares) with 240+ coordinate cells (Shade → Row → Column).
  • Quality & FEFO Priority: Lab quarantine locks defective stock; FEFO (First Expiry First Out) triggers orange priority alerts on near-expiry batches.
OPERATING FLOW: [Gate Inward] → [GRN Base Unit] → [QR Label Print] → [Lab Testing QC] → [Put-Away Cell] → [Active Stock] → [FEFO Expiry] → [QR Checkout] → [Gate Out]
